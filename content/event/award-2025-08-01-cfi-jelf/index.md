Prof. Nick Rhinehart has received a Canada Foundation for Innovation (CFI) John R. Evans Leaders Fund (JELF) award, with matching support from the Ontario Research Fund, for the project *Foundational World Models and Efficient Reward Learning for Robotics*. The award funds new robotics and compute infrastructure for the LEAF Lab, including mobile manipulation platforms and GPU servers.
